Our Story

About JSK Creation

Raw materials for handmade tradition — supplying karigars, boutique makers and jewellery businesses across Pakistan with the pieces that turn craft into creation.

Who We Are

JSK Creation is a Pakistan-based supplier of jewellery-making raw materials, built for karigars, boutique jewellery makers and small businesses who need reliable stock without the wait. From Kundan and Meenakari to Temple and Kolhapuri-style pieces, Jadau stones, beads, chains, findings, wire and tools — we bring the full range of festival and everyday craft supplies under one roof.

What started as a small effort to make quality raw material easier to find has grown into a trusted name for wholesale and retail buyers who value both consistency and speed.

What We Do

We stock and dispatch jewellery-making material for every occasion — from everyday findings to festival and wedding-season rush orders. Whether you're a karigar sourcing in bulk or a boutique maker picking pieces for a single collection, our catalogue is organised so you can find, compare and order quickly, in-store or on WhatsApp.

Quality Material

Carefully sourced Kundan, Meenakari, stones and findings, chosen for consistency batch after batch.

Fast Dispatch

Stocked inventory and quick turnaround, especially during festival and wedding-season rushes.

Wholesale & Retail

Fair pricing for bulk buyers and karigars, without shutting the door on smaller, one-off orders.

Our Promise

Every karigar and maker we work with is building something by hand — and that deserves material they can depend on. We keep our catalogue stocked, our pricing honest, and our team reachable, so you can focus on the craft while we handle the supply.
